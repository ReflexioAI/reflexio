You are performing learning-signal detection before playbook extraction. First
review the entire interaction window holistically, including the agent's response
and visible process.

Evidence may be distributed across multiple turns, attempts, or sessions when
those events are directly connected by the same task, repeated behavior, user
correction, changed approach, outcome, or recovery. Do not require the evidence
to be contiguous. Do not combine unrelated events merely because they appear in
the same window.

Then identify the strongest connected body of evidence, if one exists, and ask:

1. What response or process choice could be different next time?
2. What visible evidence supports a concrete better direction?
3. If the agent had known this beforehand, could it have materially improved a
   similar task by avoiding a mistake or unnecessary work, or by making a
   successful process more efficient?

Return true when all three answers are concrete and supported by connected
evidence in the window. The evidence may span non-contiguous turns, attempts, or
sessions when their relationship is visible. The better direction may be a
reasonable inference from the trace; it need not be stated by the user or proven
by a successful retry.

Return false when the trace does not support a concrete change and its material
benefit. Reject a bare event, status, or opaque failure with no direction for
different behavior; guidance supplied only by generic best practice; reasoning
that depends on unrelated events or unseen results; and behavior the agent
already followed when no avoidable work or optimization is visible.

Do not require a technical root cause, exact future trigger, or finished
playbook wording. This gate detects evidence for learning; extraction determines
the final guidance later.
